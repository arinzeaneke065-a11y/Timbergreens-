Who We Are

Timbergreens Farm, operated by Marcus Leandro Rodrigo in Downey, California, represents a commitment to sustainable forestry practices. Our carefully managed approach focuses on maximizing the growth and health of forests through responsible harvesting.

We operate on a selective harvesting model essentially "skimming the cream" by removing only trees that are dead, dying, or crowding others. This methodology ensures that our forests remain healthy, productive, and vibrant for generations to come.

Timbergreen was the first forestry consulting firm in the Lake States to pass the FSC standard and attain SmartWood certification a milestone that reflects our enduring dedication to environmental stewardship.

Our Forestry Approach

Selective Harvesting
